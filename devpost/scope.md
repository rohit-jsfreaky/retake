# Retake

Practise the exact words you have to say in English — and see what a listener would miss — before you record them.

## The Unique Kernel
A speech-to-text engine is a listener. Retake reads your script out of your own mouth, lines up every script word
against what the engine actually heard, and marks the words a listener would miss. Then you retake only the marked
lines, not the whole script.

## Who It's For
Someone who has to say prepared words in English when it isn't their first language: a hackathon builder recording a
demo video, a job seeker with a 60-second intro, a founder opening a sales call. Today they record the whole thing,
watch it back, feel unsure, and record it all again — with no idea which words actually didn't land.

## The Core Loop
Paste the script → read it once → see the marked script → retake only the marked lines → compare take 1 and take 2 →
the marks are gone → record the real thing. They come back before every recording that matters.

## Inspiration & Identity
Feels like a calm, serious practice tool — a teleprompter that also listens. Light, clean, professional. The script
is the hero of the screen; marks are the only strong colour. Not playful, not "AI magic".

## Why This Matters to the Learner
I record every hackathon demo myself, in English, which is my second language. I never know which words people won't
catch. I want to fix those words before I hit record, not after the judges watch it.

## What "Working" Looks Like
I paste my demo script, read it, and Retake shows (for example): one word in red because the listener heard a different
word, "basically" and "umm" as fillers, a 2-second pause before line 3, and my words per minute. I retake line 2 and line 3 only. Take 2 sits next
to take 1 — the red is gone. The "oh, that's cool" beat: the marked script after the first read, showing exactly the
words a listener would miss.

## The POC Boundary
- Paste a script (or open the sample).
- Record one read in the browser.
- Marked script: missed/misheard words, unclear words, fillers, long pauses, speed.
- Retake marked lines only; compare take 1 vs take 2 and play both.
- A simple "Ready" check.
- Sample mode: a real marked take, no mic, no signup.
- No login.

## Later
- Saved history of scripts and takes across days.
- Pronunciation tips per word (how to say "product" more clearly).
- Practise in other languages.
- A teleprompter mode that scrolls while you read.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts and login — judges and users must try it in one click.
- Live (streaming) transcription while you speak — the pre-recorded call gives the word timings we need and is simpler.
- AI rewriting your script — the point is to say *your* words better, not to change them.
- Video recording — audio is what the listener hears; the video is recorded in your normal tool afterwards.
- Mobile app — web only.
